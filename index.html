About me

Hi, I'm Turjjo (friends call me TJ), a Computer Science & Engineering student at Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology.

My first real project was a recreation of the Nokia snake game I grew up with, written in C++ with iGraphics. Since then I've built a JavaFX productivity suite, a dot-connect game from my school days, a Möbius transformation visualizer, and Signal Lab, a full audio signal processing toolkit that runs Demucs and Whisper on an Android phone, offline.

Right now I'm going deeper into Python, NumPy and Pandas for data science, and I'd love to collaborate with people who are into it too.

CSE 220 · Web + Android

Signal Lab

A complete audio DSP toolkit: FFT spectrum, spectrograms, Butterworth filtering, noise reduction, karaoke vocal separation with Demucs, lyric transcription with Whisper in English, Hindi and Bangla, plus a vault that hides audio inside encrypted PNGs. The Android version is native Kotlin and Compose and runs every model on the phone, offline.

  • Kotlin
  • Jetpack Compose
  • Python
  • ONNX Runtime
  • DSP
